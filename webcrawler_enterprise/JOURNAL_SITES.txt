Journal / OJS sites (Hamdard Islamicus and similar)
====================================================

What we found for https://hamdardislamicus.com.pk/
--------------------------------------------------
- This is an Open Journal Systems (OJS) site.
- OAI-PMH lists about **345 articles** (not 10,000+ PDFs).
- PDFs are served from URLs like:
    /index.php/hi/article/download/80/99
  (no ".pdf" in the URL).
- Each article page publishes the PDF in:
    <meta name="citation_pdf_url" content="...">
- Unique emails across the whole journal are usually a few hundred to
  low thousands — authors repeat, and many PDFs share editorial addresses.
  ~386 unique emails can be near-complete for this specific site.

What the crawler now does
-------------------------
1. Seeds OAI-PMH article list (every article view URL)
2. Seeds issue/archive pages
3. Reads citation_pdf_url from every article page
4. Treats /article/download/... as a PDF even without .pdf
5. Scans PDF bytes for emails/phones (light mode does not save files)
6. Continues page discovery even while many PDFs are being scanned

How to re-run for max contacts
------------------------------
1. Use Light mode ON
2. Start with only: https://hamdardislamicus.com.pk/
3. Fresh Start (not an old resume) so OAI seeding runs
4. Wait until Heartbeat shows docs scanned near the article count (~345)

If another site truly has 10,000+ PDFs, the same fixes apply; runtime will
be much longer. Raise Page workers / Download workers in Settings.
